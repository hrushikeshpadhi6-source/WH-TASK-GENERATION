Lateslam RCA Builder

Upload your two raw exports. The tool flags late slams (pl_create_to_stage > promise_utr), joins picker names by order ID, and builds the colour-coded hour-wise RCA — ready to copy into Google Sheets or download as Excel.

30-Minute Forecast & Manpower

Actual orders are filled automatically. Active MP is auto-counted from distinct pick users in the outbound file (editable). Type the forecast per 30-min slot — Gap % and the export columns update live.

5-Minute Drops · Forecast vs Actual

Each 30-min forecast from the Google Sheet is split into six 5-min drops. Each drop gets the whole-number share (e.g. 32 ÷ 6 = 5 each); the leftover (2) goes 1 per drop to the 5-min drops that have a late slam first, then to the next drops in time order. Drops always add back exactly to the sheet's 30-min orders & units. Actuals are counted from picklist_created_time (all orders in the store filter).

Chiller & Freezer Orders

Orders with picks from P-1-F (Freezer) or P-1-C (Chiller) locations, by 30-min slot. Orders and units per zone.
